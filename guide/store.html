Your customers' side

From first look to front door

Nine steps, designed for phones first, because that is where your customers shop.

  1. Arrive

    A fast home page with your banners, your featured products, flash deals with a live countdown, new arrivals and best sellers. Change something in your panel and it shows on the site within seconds.

  2. Find

    Search suggests products as people type. Shoppers filter by category, price, colour, size and style, and can share a link to exactly what they found. Sent on WhatsApp, a product link shows that product's picture and your store's link shows your banner.

  3. Choose

    Each product shows the right price for the chosen size and colour, and only the sizes you have in stock. Star ratings and customer reviews help shoppers make up their minds.

    Only 3 left in this size.

  4. Cart

    The cart follows signed-in customers from phone to laptop. Prices and stock are refreshed on every visit, so checkout holds no surprises. A wishlist keeps favourites for later.

  5. Delivery

    The shopper picks a state and sees the delivery fee straight away. You set the fee for each group of states. Returning customers find their address already filled in.

  6. Reserve

    The moment an order is placed, the items are held for that customer while they pay. They have 30 minutes for a card or transfer payment and 6 hours for a transfer arranged on WhatsApp. After that the items go back on sale by themselves, so your stock is always working for you. A double tap never creates a second order.

    Your order is reserved until 14:35.

  7. Pay

    Two clear buttons, and time to pay later if they need it.

    Automatic

    Pay now

    Card or bank transfer on the secure page of Monnify or Kora. The store confirms the payment by itself.

    You confirm

    Pay via WhatsApp

    A chat opens with the order number and amount already typed. The customer sends a transfer and you confirm it when the money arrives.

    Reserved

    Pay later

    The order waits with its items held. The customer can come back and pay from any device.

  8. Confirmed

    The receipt appears by itself as soon as the payment is confirmed. Customers can view it, print it or save it.

  9. Track

    Customers follow their order from placed to delivered, using the order number or the courier's tracking number. They get an email at every step.

How a payment is confirmed

Checked twice, recorded once

You never have to wonder whether an order was really paid for.

  1. The customer pays

    On the secure page of Monnify or Kora, by card or bank transfer.

  2. The payment company tells your store

    A signed confirmation arrives within moments.

  3. Your store checks again

    It asks the payment company directly and makes sure the amount matches the order.

  4. The order is marked paid

    The receipt is saved and emailed in the same step, and you are notified.

A transfer arranged on WhatsApp follows the same path with one difference. You look at your bank app and confirm the payment yourself, and the receipt goes out the moment you do.

Questions

Good to know

Can my customers pay by card and bank transfer?

Yes. They pay through Monnify or Kora, or by a bank transfer arranged on WhatsApp. The money goes into your own account.

Do customers need an account?

No. Anyone can buy and track an order as a guest. An account adds an order history and a cart that follows them across devices.

What happens when two people want the last item?

The first to order gets it and the other sees that it has just sold out. Nobody is promised something you cannot deliver.

What if someone orders and does not pay?

The items go back on sale by themselves when the time to pay ends. That is 30 minutes for a card or transfer payment and 6 hours for a transfer arranged on WhatsApp. Each customer can keep two unpaid orders at a time, and you can change that number in your settings.

Will my links show a picture on WhatsApp?

Yes. A product link shows that product's picture with its name. Your store's link shows your home page banner. It works the same on Facebook, X and iMessage.

What if a customer pays and closes the page?

The payment is still confirmed in the background and the customer receives their receipt by email.

How do delivery fees work?

You group states into delivery zones and set a fee and a delivery time for each. The fee can also grow with the number of items.

Can I run sales?

Yes. You have promo codes, flash deals with countdowns and old prices shown crossed out beside the new ones.

Can my staff have their own sign-ins?

Yes. Add a member of staff in a few taps and pause or remove their sign-in whenever you need to. Your own account is protected.

How do star ratings work?

You can give a product a starting rating. Customer reviews take over as they come in, and the number of reviews shown is always the real number.

Does the demo take real money?

No. The demo runs in practice mode so you can try checkout freely. Your own store goes live with your payment account.

Is my customers' information safe?

Yes. Card details stay with the payment company, sign-ins are protected and your records are backed up every day.

What are you adding next?

Alerts that pop up in the panel the second an order arrives, staff roles, a profile page for customers and a returns form on the order page. Today the lists refresh by themselves every minute.

How do I know it copes with a crowd?

We tested it with crowds of shoppers before putting it online. The results are in the proposal.

What do I need to get started?

Your brand name and logo, your products and your delivery fees. The proposal has the full list.
