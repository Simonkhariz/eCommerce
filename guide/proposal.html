How we launch it

Eight steps from yes to your first order

You make the decisions. We do the setting up.

  1. Together

    A friendly first call

    We agree your package, your domain name, how you deliver and how you like to be paid. You get a short list of what to send us.

  2. We do this for you

    Your accounts

    We open your domain, hosting, payment and email accounts in your name. You hold every password.

  3. We do this for you

    Your brand

    Your name, logo and colours go into the store, its emails and its receipts.

  4. You send, we add

    Your products

    We add your products with their sizes, colours, prices, stock and photos, and set up your delivery fees.

  5. Together

    Practice orders

    We place practice orders with you on a phone, so you see exactly what your customers will see and every email they will receive.

  6. We show you how

    Training

    A hands-on session on adding products, packing orders, confirming transfers, running a sale and reading your reports.

  7. Together

    Launch day

    Your store opens on your own domain and we are with you for the first real orders.

  8. We stay with you

    Support

    You have 30 to 90 days of support after launch, depending on your package, and a care plan if you would like one after that.

What we need from you

A short list to get started

Most brands have these ready already. We help with anything you are missing.

  • Your brand name and logo

    And your colours, if you have them.

  • Your products

    Names, prices, sizes, colours, stock and photos. A spreadsheet and a folder of pictures is perfect.

  • Your delivery fees

    The states you deliver to and what each one costs.

  • Your business details and bank account

    The payment company asks for these when it opens your account. We guide you through the form.

  • How customers reach you

    A support email, a phone number and your WhatsApp number.

  • The domain name you would like

    For example yourbrand.com.ng. We check that it is free.

  • One person to say yes

    Someone who can approve the look and the practice orders, so things keep moving.

  • Your policies

    Returns, refunds and delivery times in your own words.
