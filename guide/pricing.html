Good to know

Simple and fair

When do I pay?

For Launch and Growth you pay 60% to begin and 40% when your store is ready to go live. For Scale it is 40% to begin, 30% when the store is ready for you to test and 30% when it goes live.

Is hosting included in the package price?

Hosting and your domain name are paid straight to the provider, in your name, which keeps the store fully yours. For a Launch store they come to about ₦3,000 a month together. We set both up with you and tell you when each renewal is due.

How do the bills reach me?

Each provider emails its bill to you and you pay it online with your card or a bank transfer. Hosting is billed monthly or yearly, whichever you prefer. Your domain is billed once a year. The payment company simply keeps its fee from each sale, so there is no separate bill for that.

Who owns the store?

You do. Your domain, hosting, payment account, emails and customer list are opened in your name, and you hold every password.

Can I move up later?

Yes. You pay the difference between the two packages and everything moves with you.

What do I arrange myself?

Your product photos, your courier and your adverts. We are happy to advise on all three.

How long do these prices hold?

For 30 days from the day you receive this page. Your written quote then fixes the price for your project.

What do you need from me to begin?

Your brand name and logo, your products with prices and photos, and your delivery fees. The proposal has the full list.
